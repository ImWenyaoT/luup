# The knee in the cosmic ray energy spectrum

- **arXiv**: [0906.3949](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.3949)
- **Authors**: A. D. Erlykin, A. W. Wolfendale
- **Published**: 2009-06-22T08:26:19Z
- **Categories**: hep-ph (hep-ph, astro-ph.HE)
- **Comment**: Proc. 31st Int. Cosm. Ray Conf., Lodz, Poland, 4 pages, 3 figures, ID 0301

## Abstract

An update of the status of the knee in the cosmic ray energy spectrum at 3-4 PeV is presented. We argue that the evidence in favour of the presence of a 'single source' is even stronger than before.
